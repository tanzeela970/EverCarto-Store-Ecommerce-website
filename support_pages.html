Shipping Information

Delivery & Shipping

We ship to all major cities and towns across Pakistan. Orders are processed within 1–2 business days.

🎉
Free Shipping on All Orders
EVERCARTO offers free standard shipping on every order, no minimum required. We believe great products should reach you without any extra cost.

How It Works

1
Place Your Order
Once your payment is confirmed, you'll receive an order confirmation email with your order number within a few minutes.
2
Processing
Our team carefully packs and quality-checks your items within 1–2 business days. You'll receive a dispatch notification once your order leaves our warehouse.
3
In Transit
Your order is handed to our courier partner. You'll receive a tracking number via SMS and email to follow your delivery in real time.
4
Delivered
Your order arrives at your doorstep. Our courier will attempt delivery twice before returning to the nearest hub for collection.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking SMS and email containing your consignment number. You can use this to track your delivery on the courier's website.

We partner with TCS, Leopards Courier, and M&P Express for nationwide delivery. The assigned courier will be shown in your dispatch confirmation.

Shipping Issues

My order hasn't arrived

If your order has exceeded the estimated delivery window, please first check your tracking number. If no movement has been recorded for 48+ hours, and we will investigate with the courier.

Delivery attempted while I was out

Our couriers will attempt delivery twice. If both attempts are unsuccessful, your parcel will be held at the nearest courier hub for 5 business days. After this, it may be returned to us.

My address was incorrect

If your order has not yet been dispatched, contact us immediately and we can update your address. Once dispatched, address changes are subject to courier fees.

We want you to love every purchase. If something isn't right, we make returns simple.

How to Initiate a Return

1
Request a Return
Email us at returns@lumiere.pk or fill the contact form with your order number and reason for return. We'll respond within 24 hours with approval and instructions.
2
Pack the Item
Securely repack the item in its original packaging if possible. Include a note with your order number inside the parcel.
3
Ship It Back
Drop the parcel at your nearest courier hub. We'll provide a prepaid return label for defective or incorrect items. For change-of-mind returns, standard courier rates apply.
4
Receive Your Refund
Once we receive and inspect the item (1–2 business days), your refund will be processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Exchanges

We offer free size or colour exchanges on clothing and accessories within 30 days. To request an exchange, follow the same return process and indicate your preferred replacement item in your request email.

Exchanges are subject to stock availability. If your preferred option is unavailable, we will issue a full refund.
